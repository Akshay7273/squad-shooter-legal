Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026 · Applies to Squad Shooter for Android

1. Summary

Squad Shooter is a single-player game. We do not require an account to play, we do not collect personal information, and we do not sell personal information. The game stores your progress locally on your device.

2. What we collect

We do not collect personal information. The game does not ask for a name, email address, phone number or any other identifier that identifies you personally.

Your game progress (level, currency, upgrades, settings) is written to your device's private application storage. This data never leaves your device unless you move it yourself through the operating system's backup or transfer features.

3. Advertising

The game displays advertising through third-party ad networks. When an ad is requested, the ad network may receive technical information such as:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (a resettable identifier such as Google's AD_ID or Apple's IDFA)
  • Your device model, operating system version, app version and screen resolution
  • Approximate location derived from the IP address
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)
  • Your interests inferred from ad interactions

Our advertising partners include AdMob (Google), AppLovin, LevelPlay (Unity) and Unity Ads. Each provider operates under its own privacy policy. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings, and you can opt out of personalised ads in the game's settings panel.

4. Consent and tracking

Where required by law (for example the EEA/UK, and United States state privacy laws), the game presents a consent flow (Google UMP) and an App Tracking Transparency prompt before personalised advertising or tracking is enabled. Declining consent results in non-personalised advertising.

5. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing (or the App Store on iOS). We never see or store your payment card details. Google Play handles payment, fraud prevention and refunds. Purchase receipts are validated server-side by the platform before any in-game content is granted.

6. Analytics and crash reporting

The game may record anonymous gameplay analytics (for example, which levels are played and which weapons are used) and crash reports, to improve the game. These records contain no personal information and are not linked to an advertising identifier.

7. Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, no special handling applies. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the platform's account or purchase system, please contact us and we will remove it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, delete, correct or port your personal data, to object to processing, or to withdraw consent. Since we do not collect personal information, most requests are satisfied directly on your device by clearing the application's data or uninstalling it.

9. Data retention

Local game data stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall the game. We do not maintain servers that store personal information about you.

10. Third-party services

  • Unity Engine (game runtime)
  • Google Play Billing / Google Mobile Ads / Google UMP
  • Unity Ads, AppLovin, LevelPlay
  • Firebase (crash reporting and analytics, where enabled)

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be announced in the game's release notes.

12. Contact
